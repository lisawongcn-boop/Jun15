# Smelly Bag: cartoon video plan

A cute, silly cartoon music video. Sung to the "Smelly Cat" tune (from *Friends*) with new "Smelly Bag" lyrics.
Soundtrack: an AI-sung version from Suno (no need to record your voice). Visuals: short AI video clips, one per lyric line.
Put it together in CapCut. Keep it private or share it on WhatsApp, because the tune is copyrighted.

## Step 2: video clips (one per line, about 8 seconds, 9:16 vertical)

Paste one prompt per clip and attach the matching character picture(s). Every prompt starts with the same style line, so the clips match:

**STYLE:** *Cute kawaii flat-vector 2D cartoon with soft shading and no heavy outlines (matching the Andreas reference), pastel colours with lavender accents, bouncy squash-and-stretch animation, cosy and funny, 9:16 vertical, no text or words on screen.*

1. **Intro (instrumental)** - STYLE. A cosy little coffee-shop stage with fairy lights and a brick wall. The singer sits on a stool tuning her guitar. Next to her on a second stool, the cute cream tote bag with the red heart patch sits sadly, with wobbly pale-green stink lines floating up and one tiny cartoon fly doing loops above it. The camera slowly pushes in.
2. **Chorus 1: "what did he do to you... it's not your fault"** - STYLE. The singer strums and sings sweetly to the bag. The bag's huge eyes fill with wobbly tears, and it sniffles. She gently pats its handle. Little pastel music notes float up and politely swerve around the green stink lines.
3. **"He wedged you tight beneath his arm, and swore you'd come to no real harm"** - STYLE. Flashback with a soft dreamy glow. Andreas (chibi, brown quiff, big black square glasses, beige T-shirt, navy shorts) jogs up, sweaty and beaming, and squashes the tote bag tight under his armpit. The bag's cheeks puff out like a squished hamster and its eyes bulge. He winks at the camera and gives a big thumbs-up. A cartoon sparkle "ding!"
4. **"But he'd stuffed his sweatshirt in the middle, and now you smell like a warm gym riddle"** - STYLE. Andreas crams a damp grey sweatshirt into the bag on top of neat little books. A green puff cloud bursts out, and the tiny books inside faint dramatically. The bag turns pale green with swirly dizzy eyes. The stink cloud curls into the shape of a big floating question mark.
5. **Chorus 2: "...I blame the pit"** - STYLE. Back on the coffee-shop stage, the singer points the neck of her guitar like an accusing finger. A spotlight snaps onto Andreas in the audience, who slowly lowers his raised arm and whistles innocently while little stink lines drift from his armpit. Other cute audience members pinch their noses.
6. **"Phenomenal's the word he used, I'd go with 'damp' and 'slightly bruised'"** - STYLE. Andreas presents the bag like a game-show host, with confetti and sparkles, looking extremely proud. Cut to the bag: droopy handles, a small damp patch and a tiny pink plaster on its corner, blinking slowly. The singer gives a flat, unimpressed side-eye to camera.
7. **"You used to smell of books and shopping..."** - STYLE. A dreamy memory bubble: the happy bag skips through a sunny market and bookshop, swinging a baguette, flowers and a stack of paperbacks, with hearts and sparkles floating out of it. Everything is sunny and adorable.
8. **"...now you smell like a bloke who's been jogging"** - STYLE. The memory bubble pops. A green stink cloud shaped like a little man in running shorts jogs out of the bag, does a stretch and jogs off screen. The bag watches in horror with its mouth open.
9. **"You've carried paperbacks and prose, and every charity find I chose"** - STYLE. A cute montage: the proud bag carries a tower of paperbacks, a chipped floral teapot, a knitted jumper and a vinyl record out of a charity shop, glowing with happiness and puffing out its chest, with tiny sparkles all around.
10. **"But one sweatshirt beneath his arm undid a lifetime's bookish charm"** - STYLE. In slow motion, a grey sweatshirt drops from the sky like a meteor onto the happy bag. The sparkles fizzle out one by one and turn into green stink lines. The books inside cover their noses. The bag's proud smile wobbles into a tiny sad frown.
11. **Big finish: "Smelly Bag, Smelly Bag, what did he do to you?"** - STYLE. The coffee-shop stage in a warm spotlight. The singer belts it out, the audience sways with phone lights, and the bag stands on its stool as the star. Its stink lines turn into a musical stave with bouncing notes.
12. **"...he's sleeping on the sofa"** - STYLE. Night at a cosy home. Andreas is curled up on a tiny sofa that is far too small for him, with a tiny blanket and the smelly sweatshirt as his pillow, and his little navy shoes sticking off the end, his glasses askew. Pan to the bedroom: the freshly washed, sparkling-clean bag is tucked into the big bed with a little sleep mask, smiling smugly.
13. **Outro** - STYLE. Sunny morning. The clean bag hangs on a washing line, swaying happily in the breeze with its heart patch shining. A little bird lands on its handle, and they both smile. Soft bokeh sparkles.

Suno gives you two versions each time, so pick the cutest. It writes its own tune, so it won't sound like "Smelly Cat". If you want the actual "Smelly Cat" tune, skip Suno: use a soft ukulele track from CapCut's music library, and anyone who knows the song will sing the captions along to it.

## Step 4: put it together (CapCut)

1. Import the Suno song as the audio.
2. Drop the clips in order and trim each one to its line.
3. Add the lyrics as captions (Text → Auto captions, or type them). Use a round, bubbly font in white with a pastel outline.
4. Add a title card, "SMELLY BAG 🎒💨", and an end card, "The End (he's still on the sofa)".
5. Optional sound effects: a *boing* at the squish, a *poof* at the sweatshirt, a *pop* at the bubble.

Tips: don't ask the video app for words on screen, because they come out garbled; add all text in CapCut. If a clip looks off, regenerate it with the same character picture attached rather than rewriting the prompt.
