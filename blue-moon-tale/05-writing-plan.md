## Rules for drafting days
- 1,000 words means 1,000 rough words. Do not polish. Do not reread yesterday.
- If a day's part runs short, keep writing into the next part rather than stopping.
- If you are stuck, write the scene as dialogue only, then add the rest.
- Note ideas for earlier parts in `07-revision-notes.md`, not in the draft.
