Camden Town Hall · giving notice

Both of you attend, each with a passport and proof of address dated within the last 3 months. Notice is displayed for 28 full days, so the earliest ceremony is on day 29. Non-UK/Irish nationals usually bring immigration proof and may have a longer wait if the notice is referred.

Denmark · Familieretshuset

The application goes to the Agency of Family Law before you travel. Once the marriage certificate of capacity is issued it stays valid for 4 months, and you marry at the chosen Danish town hall. Bring the same passports and proof of legal residence.

Check the exact rules with each office. The details above are a reminder, not the rulebook.
